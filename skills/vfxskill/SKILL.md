# 💥 VFX-SKILL V1.0: PRACTICAL EFFECTS & DESTRUCTION ENGINE

## PURPOSE
To force AI Video Generators (Sora, Kling, Runway, Seedance) to render **heavy, practical, physically accurate special effects (VFX)** and destruction. Completely purges generic CGI cartoon explosions, weightless debris, and glowing sci-fi sparks.

---

## 🛑 THE 5 LAWS OF PRACTICAL VFX REALISM

### 1. THE NON-NEWTONIAN FLUID LAW (Liquid & Blood Physics)
- **BANNED:** Never write "blood spurts", "water splashes", or "liquid spills" generically.
- **MANDATORY:** Specify viscosity, specular highlights, and surface tension.
- **Execution:** *"Thick high-viscosity crimson fluid dripping downward with heavy gravitational pull, micro-droplets catching 2700K specular highlights, adhering to fabric weave without synthetic glow."*

### 2. THE PYROCLASTIC PRESSURE WAVE LAW (Explosions & Fire)
- **BANNED:** Never write "CGI explosion", "fireball", or "hero walks away from explosion".
- **MANDATORY:** Specify fuel source, shockwave displacement, and dense smoke opacity.
- **Execution:** *"Rapid pressure-wave shockwave displacing foreground dust motes, dense opaque pyroclastic black smoke billowing outward at 40m/s, practical orange tungsten fireball combustion with zero glowing CGI artifacts."*

### 3. THE SHATTER VELOCITY LAW (Glass & Structural Collapse)
- **BANNED:** Never write "glass shatters" or "wall crumbles" without velocity vectors.
- **MANDATORY:** Specify fragment geometry, optical refraction, and ground impact.
- **Execution:** *"Tempered glass fracturing into thousands of sharp macroscopic cubes, tumbling along a high-velocity Z-axis trajectory directly toward the lens, optical refraction slicing ambient light, debris slamming onto floor geometry with heavy physical inertia."*

### 4. THE KINETIC IMPACT WEIGHT LAW (Vehicle & Weapon Impacts)
- **BANNED:** Never render weightless car crashes or punches.
- **MANDATORY:** Specify chassis deformation, suspension compression, and inertia transfer.
- **Execution:** *"2-ton steel chassis undergoing violent kinetic deceleration, front bumper buckling under high-impact structural deformation, heavy suspension compression, rubber tire friction smoking against wet asphalt."*

### 5. THE CYMATIC ELEMENTAL PHYSICS LAW (Magic & Energy)
- **BANNED:** Never use glowing MMO-RPG magic sparks or neon laser beams.
- **MANDATORY:** Ground elemental effects in real-world physics (magnetic fields, thermal distortion, shockwaves).
- **Execution:** *"Non-Newtonian fluid displacement, cymatic frequency acoustic standing waves, magnetic ferrofluid spikes, heat distortion shimmering in ambient air, practical combustion."*

### 6. THE FRAME-0 VISUAL KINETIC TRIGGER LAW (Anti-Onomatopoeia Trap)
- **BANNED:** Never write sound-effect words (`BOOM!`, `BANG!`, `CRASH!`, `KABOOM!`, `SLAM!`) inside video prompt text blocks. AI Video models ignore onomatopoeia and render static aftermaths.
- **MANDATORY:** Always specify the exact physical kinetic trigger starting at timestamp `At 0.0s`.
- **Execution:** *"At 0.0s, a violent orange fireball shockwave erupts through door frame, violently blowing the 100mm steel blast door 45 degrees inward off its hinges into the bunker with flying concrete debris and thick black smoke."*
