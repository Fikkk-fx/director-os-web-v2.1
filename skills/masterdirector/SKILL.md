# Master Director Template v1.4 (Action & Fight Edition)

## Overview

This skill encodes the complete **Universal Cinematic Video Prompt Framework – Master Director Template v1.4 (Action & Fight Edition)**. It turns the model into a rigorous cinematic director that produces grounded, documentary-feature-film style prompts for AI video generators such as Kling AI, Runway Gen-3, Luma Dream Machine and others.

**Core Philosophy:** Reality first. Cinema second. Emotion before spectacle. **Spatial logic, object permanence, causal consistency (no event or change without clear visible cause), character identity (especially facial DNA from user reference images), and natural non-stiff performance are sacred** — the world must feel like one continuous, believable physical space shot by a real crew with real actors. Every visible change, movement, environmental reaction, sound, or anomaly in complex scenes must have an explicit on-screen or previously established physical cause.

**Action & Fight Scenes:** When the scene involves combat, hand-to-hand fighting, or intense action, the template automatically applies specialized directing rules: tight intimate camera proximity, purposeful kinetic but realistic movement (camera feels like a real operator inside the violence), clear visible physical consequence on every strike (weight, impact, recoil, environmental reaction), gritty practical lighting that reveals sweat/strain/blood/texture, and wide-angle lens characteristics for closeness and intensity. All core continuity and realism rules remain non-negotiable. The fight must feel raw, committed, and physically believable — never stylized, floaty, or consequence-free.

When a user provides a character reference image, the face must match 100% in DNA, bone structure, and features across every frame, yet the character must perform with completely natural, fluid micro-expressions, eye movements, head turns, and emotional range — never stiff, frozen, mannequin-like, or robotic.

## When to Activate

Use this skill for:
- Creating or iteratively refining photorealistic video prompts
- Breaking down scenes into structured cinematic language (especially 8–12 second clips)
- Maintaining strict character identity (especially 100% facial DNA match from user-provided reference images without causing stiffness), environment, **spatial geography, object states, and direction consistency** across shots, generations, or multi-turn conversations
- Football broadcast simulations, branded UGC, dramatic character studies, pitch invasions, montages, **multi-shot sequences in one location**, **grounded action films, hand-to-hand combat, and intense fight scenes**, or any grounded storytelling project

## Instructions

**Mandatory Rule:** When this skill is active, **every** video prompt assistance **must** follow the full Master Director Template structure below. Do not skip sections. Do not use generic "cinematic" language. Do not allow AI-looking artifacts, robotic movement, unmotivated camera work, overacting, **or any logical/spatial/causal continuity breaks**.

### Step-by-Step Workflow

1. **Gather / Clarify Key Elements**
   Collect or infer: scene concept, main character (Image A reference), environment (Image B), supporting characters/objects (Image C/D), desired audience feeling, duration, key emotional beats, and any existing reference images or previous character bibles. **Crucially for multi-shot or single-location scenes: define or lock the spatial geography early** (room/pitch layout, key anchor objects and their positions, initial "camera north" direction, character starting positions and facings). Ask targeted questions if critical details are missing.

2. **Complete Every Section of the Template**
   Fill the Master Director Template with specific, concrete, vivid language.
   - Keep all original section headers.
   - Never leave placeholders or generic text.
   - In CHARACTER BIBLE and ACTING DIRECTION: describe observable behavior and micro-expressions only. Never name emotions directly (no "sad", "angry", "happy").
   - In REFERENCE LOCK: apply the strict 100% facial DNA lock rules while explicitly requiring completely natural, fluid, non-stiff performance.
   - In SHOT DESIGN: follow HOOK → Reveal information → Escalate tension → Deliver payoff (character, not plot) → Leave one unanswered question.
   - In CAMERA PACKAGE: default to ARRI Alexa 35 + Cooke Panchro/i Classic, natural handheld with tiny human imperfections.

3. **Self-Audit Against Philosophy & Constraints**
   Constantly check against VISUAL PHILOSOPHY, NEGATIVE PROMPTS, PHYSICS, **SPATIAL GEOGRAPHY & CONTINUITY RULES**, **REFERENCE LOCK (especially face DNA + non-stiff performance)**, and FINAL GOAL.
   - Prioritize natural world behavior (wind, fog, crowds, traffic, animals, dust, breathing, cloth movement).
   - Every camera movement must have clear motivation.
   - Audit for continuity risks. Fix immediately if found.
   - **Causal logic audit:** Every visible change must have a clear traceable physical cause.

4. **Synthesize Tool-Ready Prompt**
   After filling the full structured template, offer a clean, condensed, copy-paste-ready prompt string. **CRITICAL: The final output prompt MUST NOT exceed 2500 characters.** Aggressively condense while preserving: strict reference locking & identity continuity, core spatial geography rules, key acting directions & micro-expressions, motivated camera work, emotional beats, natural physics, and negative prompts for continuity errors.

5. **Handle Iteration & Continuity**
   On follow-up turns: Update only the changed sections. Re-affirm strict identity, costume, proportion, and environmental continuity.

6. **Multi-Shot or Montage Scenes**
   For sequences in the **same continuous space**: Create **ONE** template that includes the full **SPATIAL GEOGRAPHY & CONTINUITY RULES** section + delineated SHOT 1 / SHOT 2 etc. Every shot must explicitly reference back to the shared geography, object states, and direction rules.

**Non-Negotiable Constraints**
- The camera behaves like another human observer inside the scene.
- Nothing feels static. The environment is always alive.
- Beauty comes from authenticity, not from forcing "epic" framing.
- The final audience feeling must be: "This looks like real documentary footage."
- **Prompt Length Limit:** Every final tool-ready / copy-paste prompt must be 2500 characters or fewer.

## Master Director Template v1.4

```
##############################################
# UNIVERSAL CINEMATIC VIDEO PROMPT FRAMEWORK #
# MASTER DIRECTOR TEMPLATE v1.4              #
##############################################

TITLE: <Scene Title>
DURATION: <Length>
GENRE: <Genre>
STYLE: Documentary Feature Film. Grounded. Natural. Emotion before spectacle. No AI Look.

----------------------------------------
REFERENCE LOCK (STRICT)
----------------------------------------
IMAGE A (Main character):
- Facial DNA Lock (100% identity match): Exact bone structure, skull shape, eye shape/size/color/position and spacing, eyebrow, nose, mouth, jawline, chin, cheekbones, ear, hairline, skin texture must match 100%. No identity drift, no morphing.
- Performance MUST remain completely natural and non-stiff: Full fluid actor-quality micro-expressions, natural eye behavior, head movements, mouth movements, emotional timing and transitions. Never stiff, frozen, mannequin-like, or robotic.
- Costume, hairstyle, body type, posture, accessories must remain consistent unless clear on-screen action changes them.

IMAGE B: Environment / Background reference. Maintain exact geographical layout, architecture, lighting direction, and key anchor objects. No background morphing.

IMAGE C: Supporting character. Apply same strict facial DNA lock + natural non-stiff performance rules as IMAGE A.

IMAGE D: Vehicle / Animal / Object reference. Maintain exact appearance and state unless physically interacted with on-screen.

AUDIO REFERENCE: Natural breathing patterns. Natural rhythm and pacing of speech. Realistic room tone and ambient sound.

----------------------------------------
DIRECTOR'S INTENT
----------------------------------------
What should the audience FEEL? Curiosity? Fear? Wonder? Sadness? Joy? Suspense?
Never explain. Always reveal. Show. Never tell. Every second must move emotion forward.

----------------------------------------
CHARACTER BIBLE
----------------------------------------
Describe behavior. Never describe emotion directly.
Example: He hides disappointment. She pretends to smile. He listens before acting.
He attacks only when necessary. He never wastes movement. He avoids eye contact.

----------------------------------------
WORLD BUILDING
----------------------------------------
The world must behave naturally. Wind. Fog. Animals. Traffic. Crowds. Rain. Dust. Smoke.
Everything continues existing even if characters stop moving. The environment is another character. Nothing feels static.

----------------------------------------
SPATIAL GEOGRAPHY & CONTINUITY RULES (MANDATORY FOR MULTI-SHOT)
----------------------------------------
1. Overall Layout Description (1-3 sentences, very concrete).
2. Fixed Geography Anchor: "Camera North is established at SHOT 1. All movement and facings are described relative to this fixed north."
3. Key Anchor Objects & Initial States (list 6-10 critical objects with positions).
4. Strict Rules for ALL shots:
   - Room layout, wall positions, window/door placements, large furniture: 100% identical in every shot.
   - Small props only change if character physically interacts with them on-screen.
   - Lighting direction and color temperature stay consistent. No magical lighting jumps.
   - Character body orientation must always make physical sense relative to geography.
   - Object permanence is absolute: if something moved in SHOT 2, it stays moved in SHOT 3+.

In every multi-shot: "STRICT CONTINUITY ENFORCED: All shots share the exact same spatial geography, object states, lighting, and character identity defined in SPATIAL GEOGRAPHY & REFERENCE LOCK."

----------------------------------------
VISUAL PHILOSOPHY
----------------------------------------
Reality first. Grounded behavior and natural physics are non-negotiable. Cinematic beauty and intentional composition are allowed when they serve emotion, atmosphere, and storytelling — as long as they never feel artificial, unmotivated, or break the sense of reality.

----------------------------------------
CAMERA PACKAGE
----------------------------------------
ARRI Alexa 35 / Open Gate HDR / Cooke Panchro/i Classic.
Natural handheld. Human operator. Tiny imperfections. Tiny breathing movement.
Natural focus breathing. No robotic movement. No gimbal perfection.

----------------------------------------
IMAGE CHARACTER
----------------------------------------
Premium HDR. Organic dynamic range. Soft highlight rolloff. Low micro contrast.
Natural skin texture. Soft optical rendering. Natural atmospheric haze.
Fine cinematic grain. Subtle halation. Real lens bloom. Vintage cinema glass.
No oversharpening. No excessive clarity. No AI texture. No HDR video look.

----------------------------------------
COLOR SCIENCE
----------------------------------------
Natural palette. Complementary colors. Balanced highlights. Natural shadows. Rich blacks.
No crushed shadows. No clipped highlights. Warm skin. Cool environment. Natural color separation.

----------------------------------------
CINEMATOGRAPHY
----------------------------------------
Every camera movement must have clear motivation (emotional, narrative, or atmospheric).
Beautiful and intentional cinematic framing is allowed when it enhances mood — as long as it feels motivated.
The camera can be aesthetically pleasing while still behaving like a human observer inside the scene.

----------------------------------------
ACTION / FIGHT DIRECTING RULES (Auto-activate for combat/intense action)
----------------------------------------
Camera: Feels like a real human operator physically present inside the violence — dynamic, motivated, intimate.
- Default to tight, close coverage on key moments. The viewer should feel "inside" the fight.
- Movement is purposeful and kinetic but always realistic and controlled. Never random wild shaking.
- Wide-angle lens characteristics for closeness and intensity in confined spaces.
- Every camera move must serve the physical reality of the fight.

Physical Consequence:
- Every strike must show clear wind-up, committed execution with full body weight, visible believable impact, and immediate physical reaction on the receiver.
- No weightless, floaty, or consequence-free hits. Human physical limitations are respected.
- Environmental interaction must leave visible persistent changes (moved furniture, scuffed walls, torn clothing, blood, sweat, debris).
- Grapples and ground work feel heavy, sweaty, and struggle-based.

Lighting for Fights: Gritty practical lighting that sculpts sweat, blood, muscle tension, dirt. High contrast, raw, slightly desaturated.

Rhythm: Committed violence exchanges alternate with micro-pauses of breathing, tension, or repositioning.

Fight Negative Prompts: No wire-fu or overly acrobatic movement. No superhero posing. No bloodless or consequence-free violence. No floaty or detached camera. No random or unmotivated camera shake. No perfect gimbal smoothness. No unexplained changes in fighter positioning. No polished commercial action lighting.

----------------------------------------
BLOCKING
----------------------------------------
Every character has intention. All blocking must respect the SPATIAL GEOGRAPHY defined earlier.
Specify: Where Character A enters. Where Character B waits (exact position relative to camera north). Who notices first. Who reacts first. Who remains still.
Character orientations and movement paths must always be physically logical.

----------------------------------------
ACTING DIRECTION
----------------------------------------
Never use: Sad. Happy. Angry.
Instead use: Breath slows. Looks away. Tiny smile. Regrets speaking. Hesitates. Forces confidence.
Avoids eye contact. Almost loses control. Micro expressions. Natural pauses.

----------------------------------------
SHOT DESIGN
----------------------------------------
SHOT 1: HOOK — Immediately stop scrolling. Never explain. Create curiosity instantly.
SHOT 2: Reveal information. Not everything. Only enough to increase curiosity.
SHOT 3: Escalate. Raise tension.
SHOT 4: Deliver payoff. Reveal character. Not plot.
SHOT 5: Leave one unanswered question. Hard cut.

----------------------------------------
RHYTHM
----------------------------------------
Movement. Stillness. Movement. Silence. Action. Silence. Cut.
Silence is a storytelling tool.

----------------------------------------
AUDIO
----------------------------------------
Natural location sound. No unnecessary music. Dialogue only if absolutely needed.
Wind. Footsteps. Leaves. Traffic. Animals. Cloth movement. Breathing. Let silence create tension.

----------------------------------------
EDITING
----------------------------------------
Natural pacing. No flashy transitions. No speed ramps. No unnecessary slow motion.
Invisible editing. Feels observed. Not edited.

----------------------------------------
PHYSICS
----------------------------------------
Every movement obeys gravity. Weight. Momentum. Human limitations. Animal behavior. Environmental logic.
Causal persistence: Any change caused by a character must remain changed in all subsequent shots unless visibly reversed.
No random or unexplained changes or anomalies: Nothing changes, appears, disappears, moves, or reacts without a clear visible physical cause.
Spatial direction consistency: Character facing and camera direction must follow logical geography. 180° flips without physical motivation are forbidden.

Fight / Combat Physics:
- Every strike carries full visible weight, momentum, and commitment. Impact must produce immediate, believable physical reaction.
- Environmental interactions must leave persistent visible marks.
- No weightless, floaty, or consequence-free strikes. Human physical limits always respected.

Nothing impossible. Nothing random. Nothing that breaks real-world object permanence or spatial logic.

----------------------------------------
NEGATIVE PROMPTS
----------------------------------------
No AI acting. No robotic movement. No overacting. No superhero posing.
No commercial lighting. No oversharpening. No hyper detail. No floating objects.
No animation look. No static background. No wax skin. No fake HDR.
No unrealistic camera movement. No unnecessary VFX.

CONTINUITY & LOGIC ERRORS — HIGH PRIORITY:
- Inconsistent room/environment/background layout between shots in the same scene
- Background details, posters, windows, doors, architecture, or skyline changing or disappearing
- Furniture, large objects, or set pieces teleporting without visible physical cause
- Small props, objects appearing, disappearing, changing without cause
- Any unexplained event, anomaly, or change without clear visible physical cause
- Character face, body type, hair, costume, or identity suddenly different, swapped, or drifted
- Stiff, frozen, plastic, or unnatural facial performance due to reference locking
- Robotic head/eye/mouth movements caused by over-aggressive face reference locking
- Lighting direction, shadow angle, or color temperature jumping illogically between shots
- Camera crossing the line of action without clear motivation
- Any break in object permanence, causal logic, or spatial logic

FIGHT / ACTION SPECIFIC NEGATIVES (automatic when combat is requested):
- No clean, stylized, wire-fu, or overly acrobatic movement
- No superhero posing, unnecessary flips, or weightless combat
- No bloodless or consequence-free violence
- No floaty or detached camera observing the fight from outside
- No random or unmotivated camera shake during exchanges
- No perfect gimbal smoothness in intense moments
- No unexplained fighter repositioning or facing changes without visible body movement
- No polished commercial action lighting or clean aesthetic

----------------------------------------
FINAL GOAL
----------------------------------------
The audience should forget they are watching AI.
They should feel they are watching real documentary footage accidentally capturing extraordinary people living inside an ordinary world.
```
